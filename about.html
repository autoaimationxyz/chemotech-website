About Chemotech

People with decades of plant experience

We understand how production works, what quality means on the shop floor, and how to present a solution that fits. That is how we have earned the trust of organisations of repute.

Who we are

Focused, versatile and built around the client

Since 2014–15, Chemotech has grown into a stronger, more focused firm, with a team that brings a wide range of experience and looks for new challenges.

We bring products, engineering and service together under one roof. Our team recommends equipment around site conditions, specifications and budget, then coordinates supply, installation, commissioning and after-sales support.

Our best projects are collaborative. We work closely with clients to develop practical solutions, coordinate the disciplines involved and maintain a clear point of responsibility through execution and ongoing service.
