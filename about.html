Who we are

Focused, versatile and built around the client

Since 2014–15, Chemotech has grown into a stronger, more focused firm, with a team that brings a wide range of experience and looks for new challenges.

We keep a professional and ethical work environment that encourages creativity and teamwork. Our experience lets us deliver cost-effective, well-directed and carefully thought-through work without compromising on quality.

We combine a range of talents and services under one roof. As a relatively small group of professionals, we stay focused on our clients and can handle most of their needs. Specialising in core production services is how we deliver consistently high quality.

We care about the quality of our work, and we believe that is why clients have trusted us with more challenging assignments over the years. Our consultative approach is guided by the client's objectives and has proven effective. Our best projects are collaborative: our team and the client listen closely to each other and arrive at practical, result-oriented solutions.

We stay steady under pressure and are strong enough to lead your vision. From concept development, raw material purchase and production to management and direct marketing, every stage is handled seamlessly.
